# Data and academic attribution

The datasets and text/image inputs in this upload were provided with the Dalarna University Business Intelligence labs. They are included as supporting educational material, not as original datasets created by Al Amin Alif. No blanket licence is applied to third-party material.

- Lab 2: startup-expansion workbook and US city population CSV from the supplied lab folder.

Course briefs, blank templates, duplicate notebook copies, other groups' work and private working conversations are not included in this upload. The notebooks retain the original Group 26 author names. No individual-contribution allocation is invented for these labs.
